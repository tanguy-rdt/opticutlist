# OptiCutList

Plan rectangular panel cuts for woodworking, from your parts list to printable cutting plans. OptiCutList runs locally in your browser, with no account or backend required.

**[Open OptiCutList](https://tanguy-rdt.github.io/opticutlist/)** 

## From parts to cutting plans

- **Organize your project** — define parts, materials and supplier panel references.
- **Optimize panel layouts** — respect thickness, grain direction, rotation, saw kerf and edge margins, with an option to prefer owned stock.
- **Inspect and refine** — explore dimensioned cutting layouts, make validated edits, and undo or redo changes.
- **Manage physical stock** — track owned panels and keep reusable offcuts generated by your cutting plans.
- **Prepare for the workshop** — preview, print or download vector PDFs with fabrication schedules and technical drawings.
- **Keep your work local** — save projects in your browser and transfer projects, catalogs and stock through JSON import/export.
