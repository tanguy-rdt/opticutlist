# User Guide

[Documentation home](../README.md) / User Guide

Use OptiCutList to plan rectangular cuts in sheet materials such as plywood and MDF. You enter the finished pieces you need; the application arranges them on panels and prepares printable plans.

## Start here

1. [Install or open OptiCutList](installation.md). The online option needs no installation.
2. [Create a project](projects.md) for the job you want to make.
3. [Choose materials and panels](materials.md). Add supplier panels or panels you already own if your library is empty.
4. [Enter your parts](parts.md), their finished sizes and quantities.
5. [Optimize the cuts](optimization.md) and review any pieces that could not be placed.
6. [Review cutting plans and make a PDF](cutting-plans.md).
7. [Keep reusable offcuts in stock](stock.md).
8. [Export your work and import saved files](files.md).

The project tabs stay available in any order. You can return to an earlier tab to make a correction; this is not a locked sequence of steps.

## A few useful words

- **Panel:** a sheet you will cut, such as a full sheet of plywood.
- **Part:** a finished rectangular piece you need to make.
- **Offcut:** a leftover rectangle you may be able to use again.
- **Catalog:** supplier panel references; these are not automatically panels you own.
- **Stock:** panels and offcuts you physically own.

All dimensions are in millimeters. Length and width describe the panel face; thickness is the third measurement.

## Where your work is saved

Online work stays in the browser on that device. An installed server keeps a separate shared set of data. The online version does not connect to your installed server or copy its data. See [installation](installation.md) and [saving copies](files.md#what-your-saved-files-contain) before changing devices or clearing browser data.

For light, dark or automatic appearance, open **Settings → Appearance** and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
